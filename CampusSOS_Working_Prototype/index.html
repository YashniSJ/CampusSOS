SAFETY GUIDE

Help & Safety

🚨 Immediate danger

If there is an immediate threat, move to a safe location and contact your campus security/emergency services according to your college policy.

📍 Location matters

Give a building, room, floor, landmark, or gate so responders can find you quickly.

🧑‍🤝‍🧑 Stay with others

When safe, stay with a trusted person and follow instructions from authorized responders.

🔐 Privacy

This prototype stores demo data in your browser. A production system should use secure authentication, encrypted storage, and access controls.
